Seven questions about your computer. One map to answer them.

Disk, network, CPU, battery, ports, startup and logs, each in its own small app. All local, no account, and one way of reading the data.

Download BussolaSee the apps

For macOS, Windows and Linux, in five languages. Packages of about 2 MB.

Each app answers a single question

They all read the same way: the heaviest start at 12 o'clock, each ring is a level, one click takes you inside. Learn one app and you know them all.

    Bussola installs everything else

    One app to install, open, update and uninstall the others, even all at once.

    1. Install BussolaOn macOS it takes one Terminal command and signing on your Mac.
    2. Choose the appsBussola only downloads from this project and verifies every package.
    3. Update when neededYou see new versions and decide when to install them.

    Small, local, yours

    No account

    They open and they work. There's no sign-up and no suite server.

    Your data stays here

    History stays on your computer for seven days and clears with one click.

    Careful by nature

    Anelli goes through the Trash, Radici never deletes anything, and system files are protected.

    Truly lightweight

    They use the system webview and its tools, without Electron or Node.
